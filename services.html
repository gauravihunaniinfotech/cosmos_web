09FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I schedule HVAC maintenance?

Most manufacturers recommend having heating and cooling equipment inspected at least once a year, and many homeowners in the Las Vegas heat schedule a check before summer. Cosmo’s Comfort Club is built around year-round maintenance, so ask our team which schedule suits your system.

When should I repair or replace my AC system?

It depends on the age of the equipment, how often it needs repair and how well it keeps you comfortable. After a diagnostic, our technicians explain your options plainly so you can decide what makes sense for your property.

What are common signs that my AC needs repair?

Weak airflow, rooms that won’t cool, unusual noises, odors, short cycling or a sudden rise in energy bills are all reasons to have the system checked. If your AC stops cooling, call our team.

How long does HVAC installation take?

The timeline depends on the type of system, the size of the property and the work involved. We’ll walk through the scope with you and confirm the schedule before work begins.

Does Cosmos offer residential and commercial HVAC services?

Yes. Cosmo’s Heating & Air Conditioning serves both homeowners and businesses across Las Vegas, Henderson and North Las Vegas.
