What is Cosmo’s Comfort Club?

Cosmo’s Comfort Club is our year-round HVAC maintenance membership, built around proactive care for your heating and cooling system and backed by the Cosmo’s Comfort Guarantee™.

What does membership include?

Membership is designed around tune-ups that help keep your system running like new, priority scheduling and exclusive member discounts. Specific plan details are confirmed by our team, so please contact us for the current membership terms.

Visit frequency depends on the membership plan you choose. Contact our team and we’ll confirm the schedule that applies to you.
